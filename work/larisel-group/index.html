One website speaking for three companies — equally clear to a customer, an investor and a future employee.

The task

Retail, construction and energy under one name, each with an audience of its own. One site had to carry all three in a single voice — without splintering into three sites.

What we built

  1. 01

    Three companies, one identity

    Larisel Park, Larisel İnşaat and Larisel Enerji — each with its own story and colour, inside one coherent brand.

  2. 02

    Three languages, one set of pages

    Turkish, English and Russian — every page in every language, always in step.

  3. 03

    The flagship, in 3D

    The construction page presents the group's flagship building as a model visitors turn in their own hands.
