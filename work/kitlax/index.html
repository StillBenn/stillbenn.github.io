Kıtlax

A client's website: a macaroni-crisp maker that shows how its snack is made — and sells it by the case.

Visit the site
kitlax.com

The task

The brand's old site said little about the product. The new one had to show how the snack is made, make the brand feel premium — and make it simple for a distributor to order.

What we built

  1. Watch it being madeWheat, the mill, the bronze die, the fryer, the seasoning and the bag — one film that plays as you scroll.
  2. Open a flavourFour flavours in four shapes, each with a page of its own and the product's details.
  3. Become a representativeA message to the company, with the cases per flavour added if the visitor likes.

How it is built

  • Seven shots rendered in Blender (Cycles): the wheat field, the steel mill rollers, the bronze die, the fryer, the seasoning, the bag sealed, the four flavours.
  • A film player that keeps the picture sharp at any scroll speed, and picks its quality by the visitor's connection.
  • The mascot holds the chosen flavour's bag; a case calculator writes the distributor's request.
  • Turkish, English and Arabic — the Arabic page reads right to left.
