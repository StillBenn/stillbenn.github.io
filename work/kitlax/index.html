Kıtlax

A client's website: a macaroni-crisp maker that shows how its snack is made — and sells it by the case.

Visit the site
kitlax.com

The task

The brand's old site said little about the product. The new one had to show how the snack is made, make the brand feel premium — and make it simple for a distributor to order.

What we built

  1. 01

    Watch it being made

    Wheat, the mill, the bronze die, the fryer, the seasoning and the bag — one film that plays as you scroll.

  2. 02

    Open a flavour

    Four flavours in four shapes, each with a page of its own and the product's details.

  3. 03

    Become a representative

    A message to the company, with the cases per flavour added if the visitor likes.
