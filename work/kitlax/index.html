Kıtlax

A client project: a macaroni-crisp brand's new website — the snack's whole journey from wheat to sealed bag told as one cinematic film, with a direct line to distributors worldwide.

Visit the site
kitlax.com

The task

What sets a macaroni crisp apart from an ordinary snack is hidden in the making: durum wheat, a bronze die, the right heat. The task was to make that craft felt from the very first second, present the brand like a premium product on the shelf — and let a distributor from anywhere in the world knock on the door in a few steps.

What we built

  1. 01

    A film of the making

    From the wheat field to the sealed bag — the mill, the bronze die, the fryer, the seasoning — one continuous film that moves with the visitor's scroll.

  2. 02

    A stage for every flavour

    Four flavours, four signature shapes — each with a page of its own: its colours, its shape and its full product sheet.

  3. 03

    A direct line to distributors

    In Turkish, English, Arabic and Spanish, a visitor from any market writes to the company in a minute — the cases per flavour already in the request.
