Larisel Café

A café website that guests use the way they use the counter — and then a little more.

Visit the café
larisel.com/cafe-3d

The task

Show what a café's website can be when it is more than a menu: a place where guests choose, order and come back.

What we built

  1. 01

    Design the cup

    Choose the drink, then the cup's colour, sleeve and lid — and see your name printed on it.

  2. 02

    Order from the table

    Scan the code on the table, order, and split the bill with friends.

  3. 03

    A coffee reading

    Send a photo of the empty cup and read what the grounds say.
