Larisel Café

A café website that does everything the counter does — and a few things no counter can.

Visit the café
larisel.com/cafe-3d

The task

Prove that a café's website can be far more than a menu: a place where guests design, order, and come back for the ritual.

What we built

  1. 01

    A cup with their name on it

    Drink, cup colour, sleeve and lid — chosen in seconds, with the guest's own name printed live on the cup.

  2. 02

    Ordering from the table

    One scan of the table's code: the order goes in and the bill splits between friends — all from the seat.

  3. 03

    A coffee reading

    A photo of the empty cup, and a detailed reading of what the grounds have to say — the ritual guests come back for.
