Emek Hospital

A client project: a private hospital's website, in three languages, where an anxious patient finds the right doctor and requests an appointment within a minute.

Visit the site
emekhastanesi.com

The task

Eighteen departments, twenty-five doctors, and patients who arrive anxious and on a phone. The site had to lead each of them to the right doctor without a single phone call — and give the hospital full control of its own content.

What we built

  1. 01

    Search that understands complaints

    A patient types a complaint, a department or a doctor's name — the answer takes shape with every letter.

  2. 02

    Appointments without the phone queue

    Department, doctor, day — the request lands straight in the call centre, which calls back to confirm.

  3. 03

    The hospital in command

    A control panel of its own: staff follow every request and update doctors and pages themselves, without waiting on anyone.
