Larisel Furniture

A furniture page that sells by letting the customer design the sofa, the armchair or the coffee table themselves.

Design your furniture
larisel.com/mobilya

The task

A product photo cannot show a sofa in twenty-four tones, or a table in eight stones and woods, each on eight finishes. The customer needed to see their own piece before ordering it.

What we built

  1. 01

    Pick the piece and its shape

    An armchair in two sizes; a sofa with two seats, three, or a chaise; a round, oval or long coffee table.

  2. 02

    Pick the materials

    Bouclé, velvet or linen in eight tones each; marble, travertine or solid wood for the table — seen up close before choosing, with ready pairings that set it all in one tap.

  3. 03

    A whole shop around it

    Dimensions drawn on the piece and two designs side by side — then a cart, a checkout with delivery and instalments, order tracking and a material library.
