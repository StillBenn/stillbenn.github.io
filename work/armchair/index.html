Larisel Armchair

A furniture product page that sells by letting the customer build the chair.

Build an armchair
larisel.com/threejs-chair-configurator

The task

A product photo cannot show twelve fabrics on eight frames. The customer needed to see their own chair before ordering it.

What we built

  1. 01

    Pick the fabric

    Twelve colours, and six ready pairings of chair, cushion and frame.

  2. 02

    Pick the frame and the size

    Four woods and four metals, from bleached oak to brass; standard or XL.

  3. 03

    See the price, then order

    The total follows every choice, and one tap sends the build to the workshop.
